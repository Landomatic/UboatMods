[h1]Torpedo Room — BETA[/h1]
[b]By Landomatic[/b]
Take control of torpedo preparation from the torpedo rooms in first-person and
station-restricted multiplayer play. Choose a tube, adjust its settings and Apply;
your physical levers still flood, drain and fire.
[list]
[*]Per-tube depth, supported speed, AZ/MZ and FAT/LUT programming.
[*]Full-circle LUT heading and 0.25 m depth increments from 1 to 14 m.
[*]Native torpedo artwork, larger instruments and a prominent Apply button.
[*]Current compartment, read-only opposite tubes, or all tubes editable.
[*]Synchronized, read-only or hidden viewing-station reports.
[*]Independent host toggle for bidirectional TDC speed sync; game knots/km/h units.
[*]Type/model hover, plus optional Happy Torpedo Time maintenance information.
[*]Optional timed dry-tube pistol preparation (30 game seconds by default).
[/list]
Press [b]T[/b] at a tube station. Configure through optional Dusty's Community Patch
or TorpedoRoomConfig.txt in UBOAT persistent data. Solo needs no multiplayer mod.
All shared-boat players require the same build (0.1.24 / TRC9). Supplied MP
integration targets Uboats: Coop Multiplayer 1.11.5 / protocol 54.
Mixed speeds/TDC mismatches warn without mod-added firing holds; existing native
readiness and MP permissions apply. FAT/LUT remains adjustable at sea.

[h2]0.1.24 Beta additions[/h2]
Hover depth-disabled Feuer for a Too deep to launch. explanation in all report modes, including Hidden Reports. Updates when depth or selection changes.
Selected Prep card has a brass outline. At depth, the UI stays available for tube selection; only Feuer is depth-blocked. Hidden/MP Feuer is 1.5x and follows the torpedo opener with spacing. Live .23 test pending.
The T5 deep-launch display now recovers when a selected non-T5 is fully drained. Dry/empty checked tubes do not block flooded T5 candidates. A flooded mixed selection still cannot use the exception. Retains the raised Feuer and optional MP-only display from .21.
Hidden Reports leaves only the native Feuer button in the launch panel. The native
fire-control boxes select its tube/salvo; flood tubes with the normal controls.
Read-only and synchronized retain native selection/dispersion/firing. Physical
TDC and direct tube levers retain native behavior. Running depth is 1–14 m in
0.25 m steps. An all-T5 selection (stock prototype included) can use the viewing
Feuer interface through 50 m of boat depth; this does not set running depth to
50 m or change engine/guidance behavior. Live .20 game/MP testing is pending.

[h2]Optional multiplayer scope firing[/h2]
The host can enable [b]MP: Show Feuer when scope tubes are restricted[/b] in Torpedo
Room settings (OFF by default). With another shared-boat player aboard and MP's
[b]TUBES PANEL AT THE SCOPES[/b] OFF, scopes/UZO automatically show Feuer alone.
This is independent of solo/global report settings. Native selection, readiness,
confirmation and MP firing transport remain. All players need .24/TRC9; adapter
targets supplied MP 1.11.5. New live MP testing is pending. Feuer is also raised
above the bottom icons. Copyright (c) 2026 Landomatic; existing license/credits.
