[h1]Torpedo Room — BETA[/h1]
[b]By Landomatic[/b]
Take control of torpedo preparation from the torpedo rooms in first-person and
station-restricted multiplayer play. Choose a tube, adjust its settings and Apply;
your physical levers still flood, drain and fire.
[list]
[*]Per-tube depth, supported speed, AZ/MZ and FAT/LUT programming.
[*]Full-circle LUT heading and 0.25 m depth increments from 0.5 to 14 m.
[*]Native torpedo artwork, larger instruments and a prominent Apply button.
[*]Current compartment, read-only opposite tubes, or all tubes editable.
[*]Synchronized, read-only or hidden viewing-station reports.
[*]Independent host toggle for bidirectional TDC speed sync; game knots/km/h units.
[*]Type/model hover, plus optional Happy Torpedo Time maintenance information.
[*]Optional timed dry-tube pistol preparation (30 game seconds by default).
[/list]
Press [b]T[/b] at a tube station. Configure through optional Dusty's Community Patch
or TorpedoRoomConfig.txt in UBOAT persistent data. Solo needs no multiplayer mod.
All shared-boat players require the same build (0.1.19 / TRC8). Supplied MP
integration targets Uboats: Coop Multiplayer 1.11.5 / protocol 54.
Mixed speeds/TDC mismatches warn without mod-added firing holds; existing native
readiness and MP permissions apply. FAT/LUT remains adjustable at sea.
