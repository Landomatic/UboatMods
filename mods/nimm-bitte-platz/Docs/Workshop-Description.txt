[h1]Nimm Bitte Platz: Hydrophone Fix[/h1]
By Landomatic | Version 1.4.0

Corrects exterior surface wash that can linger after returning to the submerged
hydrophone station. So you can stay seated and keep listening.

The correction applies only while the submerged hydrophone station owns the
audio listener. It releases its override on station exit, surfacing or loss of
ownership. Detection ranges, contacts, crew controls and camera FOV are unchanged.

[h2]Settings[/h2]
With Dusty's Community Patch installed, open Other Mods → Nimm Bitte Platz for:
[list]
[*]Hydrophone correction on/off
[*]Info, Debug or Trace logging
[*]Trace sample interval (default: two seconds)
[/list]
Changes apply live and save. Dusty is optional. Without it, use
NimmBittePlatzDiagnostics.txt in UBOAT's AppData folder and restart after edits.
New configs default to Info. Existing DIAG settings are preserved; switch an old
Trace config to Info for normal play.

[h2]Installation and scope[/h2]
Enable only one copy of the mod and allow Reflection and IO permissions.
The original repair was reported clean on Type VIIB with starter GHG. Other hulls,
equipment and mod combinations are not comprehensively validated. This is not a
fix for separate FPP vibration or brief view-change crackle.

Include the output log after quitting, game version, hull, hydrophone equipment,
enabled mods and the exact sequence that causes the sound issue.
